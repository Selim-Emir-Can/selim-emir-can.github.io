Tissue image reading study: pilot

Preliminary version for testing the survey form

This is a PILOT. It tests the survey form, its instructions and how long it takes. Answers given here are not study data and are never analysed together with the main study. The pilot uses a separate set of images that does not appear in the main study.

What you will do

You will look at fluorescence microscopy image tiles of fixed mouse brain and heart tissue. Some are chemically cleared-and-stained reference images (C&SF reference), others are virtual outputs computed by a model from an unstained image of the same field.

  • Part 1: 13 single images, six multiple-choice questions each.
  • Part 2: 26 pairs of images of the same field; pick the C&SF reference and give your confidence.
  • About 15 minutes. A desktop or laptop screen is recommended; phones work but show the images smaller.

If you may later be invited as a reader in the main study, please do not take the pilot (pilot participants cannot be main-study readers).

Saving and exporting your answers

  • Nothing is sent to a server. Your answers are kept in this browser (local storage) after every page, so you can close the tab and resume later in the same browser.
  • At the end, press Download CSV and Download JSON. A backup download is also available at the bottom of every page. If downloading does not work (some phones), press Show as text and copy the text.
  • If you were asked to test the pilot, send both files to the person who sent you this link, with any comments on clarity, display or timing.
  • Each form has a "Restart" button on its last page to clear your pilot answers from this browser.
