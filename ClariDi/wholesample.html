How these mosaics are built. Every tile is placed at its position in the original field of view. In the VS panels each tile is the output of the model trained for the fold in which that tile was held out, so the whole mosaic consists of held-out predictions (5 folds, so 5 different trained models contribute to one mosaic). Tiles were generated independently, so seams at tile borders are expected and are not stitching errors; neighbouring tiles can also come from different folds (turn on folds to see which). The 5×5 and 10×10 grids cover the same tissue at two crop sizes and are shown separately. Hatched areas have no tile at this grid (background or excluded); marks crops that contain a masked-out region. Generations sets how many independent draws (seeds 1234–1238) of each method are shown, one row per method; set it to 1 to compare a single chosen draw side by side.
Controls. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan (all panels move together), click a tile to pin its details, n/Shift+N next/previous sample, s switch grid, g 5 / 1 generations, d next draw (1 generation), 0 fit, +/− zoom.
